Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants and some other organisms use sunlight to synthesize foods with the help of chlorophyll. It occurs primarily in the leaves. The reaction converts carbon dioxide and water into glucose and oxygen. The light-dependent reactions take place in the thylakoid membranes, while the Calvin cycle occurs in the stroma. Chlorophyll absorbs mostly blue and red light. The overall equation is 6CO2 + 6H2O + light -> C6H12O6 + 6O2. Factors affecting the rate include light intensity, temperature, and CO2 concentration.
